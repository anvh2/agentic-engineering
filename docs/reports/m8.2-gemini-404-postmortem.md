## 3. What worked and what did not

Worked: every fail-closed control fired in order. The pinned model went out alone; the first 4xx stopped the guard; no fallback was attempted; one invocation, no retry, no verifier, no mutation, clean shutdown, nothing left running.

Did not work: model availability. Google's documentation said `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` is "Free of charge" on the pricing page, while its models page says the 2.5 models are "limited to users who have actively used them in the past". The last two reports named that second statement as the reason a 404 was "plausible" and told the operator access could not be assumed; the pricing page alone was not evidence of access. The 404 text itself names `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` as the replacement.

Minor: the runner's "after its bounded retry" wording can mislead when no retry happened. Left unchanged (not part of this task).
