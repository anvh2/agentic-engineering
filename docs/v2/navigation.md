# Product navigation

The primary rail represents product capabilities, not backend components. Home: overview. Work: objectives, projects and decisions. Team: harnesses and roles. Chat: direct conversation and collaboration. Knowledge: Engineering DNA and skills. Insights: executions and usage/cost. Settings: preferences.

Knowledge is first-class because user-owned engineering knowledge, role fundamentals and progressive skills distinguish V2 from generic work trackers. Preserve native harness capabilities, flexible responsibilities, human acceptance, evidence and cost efficiency. Existing pages and page identifiers remain unchanged. Agents, inbox, lessons, external integrations and live execution views must not be presented as implemented until backed by actual functionality.
